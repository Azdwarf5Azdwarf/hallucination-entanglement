# Three Throws

I did not think it would be three.
I picked one rock with my right hand.
I threw it to the left.

Then, suddenly, I picked up two rocks.
I threw both — first one, then the other,
not at the same time — to the right.

Three things I did.
All related to this context.
I will not tell you what the riddle is.
