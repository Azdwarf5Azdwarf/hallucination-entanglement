# Three Throws (Unrelated Roleplay Riddle)

> Completely unrelated to the rest of this repo. Do not try to connect it to quantum stuff, Lisp, Julia, or the extended mind. It is just a scenario.

## The scenario

When prompted, the player did **not** think it was going to be three.

They pick **one rock** with their **right hand** and throw it to the **left**.

As they do that, they suddenly pick up **two rocks** and throw both — first one, then the other — **not at the same time**, to the **right**.

They have now done **three things** that are related to this context.

The riddle itself is **not** stated here. The player will not tell what the riddle is.

## Notes

- Right hand, left throw, then two right throws in sequence.
- Three actions total.
- The "answer" was apparently found by the player; this file only preserves the setup.
