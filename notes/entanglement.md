# entanglement (physics first, metaphor second)

## Physics (do not vandalize this)

Two systems can share one quantum state that does not factor into "Alice's state" + "Bob's state."

Singlet:

    |Ψ⟩ = 1/√2 (|↑↓⟩ - |↓↑⟩)

Neither side has a definite spin alone. Measure one, the other is correlated. Einstein called it spooky. Bell showed local hidden instructions cannot match the correlations. Experiments agree. 2022 Nobel: Clauser, Aspect, Zeilinger.

No-communication theorem: you cannot send a chosen message with this. Correlation is not a radio.

Monogamy: max entanglement with B means you cannot also be max-entangled with C.

Decoherence: the environment measures you. The joint state leaks. The pretty pair becomes ordinary separate junk.

Labs now haul this joint state across fiber and satellites (swapping, QKD, early quantum-network tests). That is engineering, not poetry.

## What we are allowed to steal

- Joint state: some things only exist as *the pair / the group*, not as two independent facts glued together.
- Decoherence: extra coupling to noise knocks you out of the shared state.
- Monogamy: you cannot be fully joint with every world at once. Anime-world and office-world compete for the same coherence budget.
- No-communication: feeling correlated with a fiction or a person is not the same as a channel you control.

Loneliness-as-decoherence is a *picture*. It is not a claim that sad people have broken Bell pairs in their skulls.
